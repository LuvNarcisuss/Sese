## Your account

One-tap sign-in creates or opens a community account using a credential stored on this iPhone. Keep access to that credential to return to your account. A new device or loss of the credential can prevent recovery. Your wallet balance is kept on the purchasing device. You can edit your profile, choose whether it appears in the member directory, block other members, or request account deletion in Settings.

Diamonds are a digital balance associated with the purchasing account on this device. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred. Contact Apple for App Store billing and refund requests.
