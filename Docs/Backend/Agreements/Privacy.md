# Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

## Account and activity

One-tap sign-in uses a random credential stored in this iPhone's Keychain. Supabase stores an internal account identifier and password verifier. Sign-in does not collect a personal email address. Supabase also hosts your session, consent, profile, follows, blocks, likes, saves, comments, submissions, messages, chat photos, and reports. Hosting receives IP addresses and request metadata.

## Visibility and external services

Discoverable profiles can appear to signed-in members. Public comments and approved submissions can be seen by eligible members. Conversation participants can read messages and photos. The app resizes the selected photo and omits its original metadata before upload. Authorized operators may access data to investigate abuse. When you open a linked video or external source, that provider processes the visit under its own policies.

## Purchases

Apple processes payments. The app uses verified StoreKit transaction details to credit diamonds. The transaction identifier, purchased pack, diamond amount, and purchase time are kept with the wallet on your device. The app does not receive your full payment card number.

## Your choices and deletion

Use Settings to edit your profile, change directory visibility, manage blocks, sign out, or delete your account. Signing out or uninstalling does not itself delete cloud data. Account deletion removes your service identity, community records, and photos in your conversations. App Store records are controlled by Apple. Previously exported or independently stored copies remain outside these controls.
